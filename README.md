One job title, 60 characters at most, and nothing else — entered in the
[make.fun bounty "Weirdest Job Title: Blind Judge Showdown"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-rusdyxbt-weirdest-job-title-blind-judge-showdown-552d7922).

## The entry

> **Chief Apology Officer for Self-Checkout Machines**

A real-sounding C-suite title with one hilariously specific twist: someone
whose whole job is saying sorry on behalf of the supermarket machines that
beep at you for an "unexpected item in the bagging area".

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes the single title to
`/output/entry.txt` and prints it to stdout. It validates the title first:
non-empty, and no more than 60 characters.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
